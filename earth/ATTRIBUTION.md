# Earth asset attribution

Terrain meshes, textures, ocean colour, water maps, moon albedo, star catalogue,
and atmosphere tooling are adapted from
[Geographical Adventures](https://github.com/SebLague/Geographical-Adventures.git) at commit `82fcda20bebb033c749b2339e9ce3a6e58007699`.
Copyright 2024 Sebastian Lague, used under the MIT License. The upstream license
is reproduced in `UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt`.

Upstream credits include NASA Visible Earth / LROC for topography and moon
imagery, Natural Earth for coastline and lake geometry, HYG for star data, and
Earth Observatory night lights. These assets are for visualization, not
navigation.
